How were Earth's atmosphere and oceans formed?

Answer generated with citations from earth.pdf. Each claim links to the exact source passage it was drawn from.

Answer

Earth's atmosphere and oceans were formed by volcanic activity and outgassing.[1] Water vapor from these sources condensed into the oceans, augmented by water and ice from asteroids, protoplanets, and comets.[2]
