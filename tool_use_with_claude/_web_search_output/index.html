Schizophrenia may reflect disruptions in brain development as well as environmental factors such as prenatal or early life stress.[1] Childhood central nervous system viral infections increase the risk of psychotic illness, possibly through direct effects of pathogens and inflammatory response on the developing brain.[2] Some researchers propose that childhood traumatic events play a causal role in schizophrenia by affecting brain development.[3]
